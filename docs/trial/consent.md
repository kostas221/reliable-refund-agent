# Taking part in the trial

Read by each participant before the session starts; they agree out loud or stop there.

This is a test of a customer-support assistant for refunds, built as a portfolio project. You will play a customer
(or a staff member) of a made-up shop, using made-up accounts and orders. No real money moves and nothing is bought.

- **What you do:** four short tasks, about 45 minutes in all. There are no right or wrong answers: the test is of the
  assistant and the screens, not of you.
- **What is recorded:** the observer takes notes of what you do and say. The messages you type are sent to the
  model provider (OpenAI) through its API, to produce the assistant's answers, and are saved on the observer's
  computer for analysis. Please type nothing about yourself or anyone real.
- **What is published:** a short report with counts and short quotes. You are named only by a code (P1 to P5), never
  by name.
- **Your choice:** you may skip a task, ask a question, or stop at any time, without giving a reason.

Questions about the trial or the report go to the project's owner.
